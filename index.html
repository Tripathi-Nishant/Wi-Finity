    About Wi-Finity Digital Twin Model

    1. Physics Simulation Engine

    This application simulates 56 OFDM subcarrier amplitudes across a 2.437 GHz carrier (Wi-Fi Channel 6) with 312.5 kHz spacing. The channel frequency response is modeled as: 

    H(f) = Σ (a_p / d_p) * exp(-j * 2π * f * d_p / c)

    Multipath paths include the direct line-of-sight path, specular image-source reflections from the 4 walls, ceiling, and floor, as well as dynamic human scatterers. Human motion shifts path length, creating constructive and destructive phase interference ripples.

    2. Strict Blind Detector Honesty

    The detector has zero access to ground truth. It receives only Float32Array(56) streams per link, applying a Hampel outlier filter, sliding-window standard deviation for motion, baseline divergence for presence, and cosine similarity against 3×3 zone survey fingerprints.

    3. Non-Negotiable Honesty & Ethical Rules

    • Simulation: Clearly labeled "SIMULATION - synthetic Wi-Fi signal" at all times.
    • No Medical Claims: Evaluated strictly as a "possible fall pattern", never "fall detected".
    • No Exact Counting: Only flags "Multiple movers likely" when multiple zones diverge.
    • Hardware Extensibility: Real ESP32 or Linux 802.11n CSI packets can be streamed directly via the WebSocketSource interface.
